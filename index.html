About

Welcome! I am an economics PhD student at Indiana University Bloomington. I hold bachelor's degrees in both economics and mathematics from Kent State University.

My research interests include poverty and inequality, social welfare programs, international trade, health, and environmental economics. I am particularly interested in understanding how public policy shapes individual and household outcomes, as well as the economic consequences of trade wars.

Term Papers

  • Family Income and the Gap in Educational Attainment: Evidence from Higher Education

    April 2026

    Used PSID data and MLE regression methods to follow a cohort born from 1993-1997 to estimate the independent effect of family income on children’s educational attainment.

    Paper

  • The Strategy of Protectionism: Game Theory in International Trade

    December 2025

    Applied concepts from game theory to analyze the 2025 Trade War. Three games were considered, including an indefinite repeated prisoner’s dilemma where both countries employ grim trigger and a game with alternating offers to model trade deals made by the U.S. and an Adversary.

    PaperSlides

  • The Effects of the 2018 Trade War on the Economy: A Review of the Literature

    May 2025

    Conducted an extensive review of 14 research papers on the effects of the 2018 Trade War on welfare, consumption, employment, trade reallocation, investment, imports, and exports.

    PaperSlides

  • Economic Solutions for a Warming World: Correcting the Negative Externalities of Climate Change

    May 2025

    Compares climate policies on theory, tradeoffs, and real-world results to identify the most effective ways to cut greenhouse gas emissions.

    Paper

  • Effects of Ability and Family Circumstances on Earnings Potential

    May 2024

    Uses OLS and instrumental-variable regressions to estimate how ability and growing up with a single mother affect earnings potential.
